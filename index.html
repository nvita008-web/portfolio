About

I'm a computational chemist whose deepest work is physics-based, molecular dynamics, free-energy methods, and QM/MM, applied alongside structure-based design and cheminformatics. At Revolution Medicines I characterized reaction mechanisms, built the workflows behind them, and translated results into decisions medicinal chemists could act on. I care most about the why behind a design, and about making computational results something a chemist trusts enough to make the molecule.

Selected Work

Covalent inhibition of KRAS G12D — QM/MM (Science, 2025)

I characterized the covalent mechanism of a KRAS G12D inhibitor, mapping the SN2 reaction of the warhead engaging the mutant aspartate and showing the protein environment lowers the reaction barrier. The animation shows the forming covalent bond along the reaction coordinate.

from Weller et al., Science 2025

Free-energy methods for resistance mutations (thermodynamic integration)

I developed a TI protocol to quantify how binding-site mutations affect affinity, tuned to converge within design timelines.

Structure-guided library design

Working from interaction analysis, I directed library enumeration into productive regions of the binding pocket, filtering by physicochemical properties and synthetic accessibility with medicinal chemistry.

Scientific software, end to end

I designed and shipped a production web application with an LLM-based data-extraction pipeline and interactive analysis dashboard, from architecture through deployment.
